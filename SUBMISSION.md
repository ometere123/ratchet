# Ratchet — stable StudioNet submission

Ratchet is a standalone GenLayer Intelligent Contract primitive for append-only, non-weakening natural-language policy tracks. An owner can propose a candidate, but only a candidate independently assessed as strictly stronger than both the current head and immutable genesis can advance a sealed track. The contract records rejected attempts, enforces stale-head protection and exposes pinned consumer predicates.

## Consensus and provenance boundary

The consensus-critical fields are the relation to current and genesis, scope preservation, and the adversarial audit result. The recorded natural-language tightening witness is a bounded **leader-reported explanation**, not a claim that every validator generated byte-identical prose. `get_amendment()` exposes this provenance distinction and the bounded consensus facts explicitly.

`is_policy_at_least` counts distinct tracks with accepted tightening, so repeated revisions on one track cannot satisfy a multi-track threshold. Exact requirements should pin tracks and use `is_track_at_least`.

## Scope and limitations

Ratchet compares public, user-supplied policy texts; it does not authenticate external facts or formally prove unrestricted natural-language subset containment. Correlated model errors remain possible. There is no frontend, backend, escrow, admin override or downgrade path. The optional consumer demonstrates a narrow pinned-track gate and does not automatically apply policy to arbitrary actions.

The earlier deployments are historical only. No historical transaction is presented as evidence for the current addresses.
